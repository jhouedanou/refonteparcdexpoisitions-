CSR policy

More responsible events.

In line with the GL events group’s CSR policy, the GL events Venues network, which the venue belongs to, is committed to three priorities.

  1. 01

    Reduce the carbon footprint of events and of the site.

  2. 02

    Limit single-use items and develop the circular economy.

  3. 03

    Promote diversity and support local communities.
