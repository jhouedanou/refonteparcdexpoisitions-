Services

More than a venue: a complete event experience.

The Abidjan Exhibition Center supports its clients in designing and delivering their events, providing modern infrastructure, flexible venues and a complete range of services.

    Full cleaning of the Convention Center and the Exhibition Hall at the end of the event

    Waste removal (7 m³ or 20 m³)
    Optional: outdoor restroom attendants, additional staff

Event services

A team by your side, from planning to the big day.

Because an event’s success depends as much on the quality of the venue as on its organization, we have developed a range of services designed to meet the requirements of organizers, companies, institutions and partners, whatever the format, size or nature of their event.

From catering to translation and interpreting, including technical and audiovisual solutions, security, reception, signage, logistics and operational support, our teams and partners put their expertise at the service of every project.

Our ambition: to let you focus on what matters most: your event.

CSR policy

More responsible events.

In line with the GL events group’s CSR policy, the GL events Venues network, which the venue belongs to, is committed to three priorities.

  1. 01

    Reduce the carbon footprint of events and of the site.

  2. 02

    Limit single-use items and develop the circular economy.

  3. 03

    Promote diversity and support local communities.
