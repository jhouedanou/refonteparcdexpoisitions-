The Dome

The venue’s Convention Center: a 5,000 m² hall equipped for conferences, institutional events, trade shows and concerts.

Key factsGalleryOverviewEquipmentRelated servicesDocuments
  • 5,000 m²Usable floor area
  • 4,800Seats, convention layout
  • 9,353Seated/standing capacity

A prestigious hall for major events.

With 5,000 m² of usable space, the Dome holds 4,800 seated guests in convention layout, 5,183 for a gala dinner and up to 9,353 in a seated/standing layout, with technical infrastructure designed for professional and public events alike.

Its auditorium with retractable seating suits congresses, conventions, trade shows, conferences and gala dinners.

The Dome has 8 artists’ dressing rooms and 2 organisers’ offices.

The Pas Perdus, a 581.5 m² reception area, holds up to 160 people.

Recommended uses
