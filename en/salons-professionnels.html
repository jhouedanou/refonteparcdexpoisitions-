Hosting a trade show

Your trade show in Abidjan.

A modular 6,500 m² hall, esplanades for large formats and integrated services: the venue hosts West Africa’s leading trade shows.

Why the venue

Three reasons to choose the venue.

  • 6,500 m² in a single space

    The exhibition hall, with 17 m clear height, can be split into 2 or 3 areas for one or several trade shows.

  • Outdoor space for large formats

    67,000 m² of forecourts and esplanades for vehicles, machinery and open-air demonstrations.

  • One point of contact

    Set-up, furniture, audiovisual, catering, security and cleaning: our teams coordinate every service.
