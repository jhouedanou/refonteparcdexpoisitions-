Destination

Coming to Abidjan.

Access, accommodation, formalities: everything you need to welcome attendees from the region and around the world.

  • Getting to the venue

    The venue is on Boulevard de l’aéroport in Port-Bouët, just minutes from Félix-Houphouët-Boigny International Airport.

    More than twenty airlines connect Abidjan with Africa and the rest of the world.

    See the access map

  • Where to stay

    Abidjan offers a wide range of hotels, from business hotels in Le Plateau to establishments in Marcory, Riviera and Port-Bouët, close to the venue.

    Our team can advise you according to the size of your delegation.

  • Visas and formalities

    ECOWAS nationals enter Côte d’Ivoire without a visa. Other travellers apply for a biometric visa online before departure.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the venue far from the airport?

No: the venue is on Boulevard de l’aéroport in Port-Bouët, just minutes from Félix-Houphouët-Boigny International Airport.

Do I need a visa to visit Côte d’Ivoire?

ECOWAS nationals do not. Other travellers apply for a biometric visa online before departure.

Can the venue help accommodate a delegation?

Our team can point you to hotels suited to the size of your delegation and your budget.

Which currency is used?

The CFA franc (XOF). International bank cards are accepted in most hotels.
